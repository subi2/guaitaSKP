guaitaSKP

A 3D viewer for SketchUp (.skp) files in your browser: open models, navigate them, select their elements, switch layers on and off, cut them with a section box and see them with SketchUp's sky and ground look.

01 · Introduction

What guaitaSKP is

guaitaSKP reads .skp files without needing SketchUp. It rebuilds the geometry, the material colours and the real layers (called tags in recent SketchUp versions) of the file, and shows them in a 3D viewer designed to look like SketchUp's: sky in the background, ground at elevation 0 and the red/green/blue axes triad.

Interface language. The viewer comes in two builds with the same code: guaitaSKP 0.14.html (Catalan interface) and guaitaSKP-en 0.14.html (English interface). This manual quotes menu and button names as they appear in the Catalan build, in italics, followed by their English equivalent in brackets, which is what the English build shows (the wording may differ slightly); the full list is in The interface.

Real elements

Each SketchUp group or component can be selected as a whole, with all its materials.

The file's layers

A list with colour and element count; switch them on and off with one click.

Section box

Cut the model along X, Y and height, with rotation and a dark poché cap at the cut.

SketchUp axes

X red, Y green, Z (vertical) blue, at each file's internal origin.

02 · Before you start

Requirements and privacy
  • A modern browser with WebGL (recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
  • An Internet connection when the viewer loads: Three.js and the fonts are downloaded from a CDN. The engine that reads the .skp files (OpenSKP) is embedded in the file itself.
  • Your files never leave your computer. .skp files are processed locally in the browser; they are not uploaded anywhere.
Good to know. Unlike other tools in the Guaita suite, this viewer does not work completely offline, because it depends on the Three.js CDN.

03 · Loading

Opening models

You can open one or several files at once. Only files with the .skp extension are accepted: others are ignored and, if none is .skp, a warning appears.

  1. Choose how to open them: menu Fitxer → Obre fitxer(s) .skp… (File → Open .skp file(s)…), the O key, the + Obrir .skp… (+ Open .skp…) button in the Models panel, a click on the empty start screen, or simply drag them onto the viewer.
  2. While each file is analysed, a loading indicator shows its name.
  3. When finished, the camera frames everything loaded and the model appears in the Models panel.

If a file cannot be read, a message explains why and the rest of the files in the same batch keep loading.

Model position. Each model is centred on its own: horizontally around the origin, and with its base exactly at elevation 0 (on the ground). If you open several, they overlap at the centre; the relative position they may have had to each other is not preserved.

To remove models: the ✕ on each card (asks for confirmation) or Fitxer → Buida el visor (File → Clear the viewer).

07 · Tags

Layers

Each card has a Capes (Layers) drop-down with all the file's layers that contain something. Each row shows:

  • a checkbox to show or hide the layer;
  • the layer's colour, as defined in the file;
  • the layer's name;
  • the number of elements (groups or components) in it.

Unchecking a layer hides all its elements instantly, without reading the file again.

Layers hidden in the file. Older files (SketchUp before 2021) store which layers were switched off, and guaitaSKP respects that on opening. Modern files do not expose this information, so all their layers appear visible.
Show all and layers. Mostra-ho tot (Show all, X) makes every hidden element visible again, including those of unchecked layers, but in this version the checkboxes in the list are not re-ticked automatically. If that happens, tick them by hand.

08 · Elements

Select, isolate and hide

Selecting

  • Click on an element: the whole group or component is selected (not a single face) and highlighted in lime green.
  • Shift + click: adds or removes elements from the selection.
  • Click on empty space or Esc: deselects.

A click only counts if you have not dragged, so orbiting the view never changes the selection.

09 · Cuts

Section box

Cuts the active model with an adjustable box, and caps the interior faces the cut leaves exposed with a dark poché, as in an architectural section.

  1. Open it from Eines → Caixa de secció (Tools → Section box), with the ⛶ icon in the views box, or with Secciona… on a model's card.
  2. Pick the model in the drop-down at the top (if you have more than one) and tick Activa (Enable).
  3. Move the sliders to cut.
SliderWhat it does
Gir (Rotation)Rotates the box from 0° to 360° around the vertical axis.
XTwo sliders: minimum and maximum limit in that direction.
Y (planta) (Y, plan)Two sliders: limits in the perpendicular direction, seen in plan.
Alçada (Height)Two sliders: cuts from below and from above.
↺Resets all values.

Values are percentages of the model's own bounding box. With a section active, the plan and elevation views (Visualitza → Càmera) frame the section box instead of the whole model.

10 · Presentation

Views and appearance

Views box

The round icon at the top right of the viewer opens a small box with:

  • Views: PL plan, N, S, E, O elevations (north, south, east, west; O stands for oest, west), and ⌂ axonometric. In the English build these read TOP, N, S, E, W.
  • ⤢ fit · ◳ switch between perspective and orthographic · ⛶ section box.
  • Fons (Background; labelled BG in the English build): sky, white, dark grey and black.

The same options are in Visualitza. The chosen background is remembered by the browser for next time; by default it is SketchUp's sky.

Wireframe and transparency

Visualitza → Filferro (Wireframe) shows the triangle edges; Visualitza → Transparència (Transparency) makes all models translucent. They can be combined.

Ground and axes

  • Ground: a solid plane at elevation 0 with a soft grid. On by default; Visualitza → Terra hides it. Models' bases rest on it.
  • Axes (RGB): a SketchUp-style triad — X red, Y green, Z (vertical) blue. Each model carries its own, placed at the file's internal origin (where the author had the origin in SketchUp), and it moves with the model. On by default; Visualitza → Eixos (RGB) hides them.

12 · Being upfront

Known limitations
  • .skp only. It does not open any other format.
  • No textures. Each face is painted with the flat colour of its material; texture images are not shown.
  • Reverse-engineered reading. The .skp format is not public, so OpenSKP rebuilds it with no guarantee of full coverage. Tested here with files from SketchUp 2017 to 2020; files from 2021 onwards are supported by OpenSKP but have not been tested in this viewer. Some older file with an unusual combination may fail with an error message.
  • Several models: their relative position is not respected (see Opening models).
  • Inactive buttons: on the toolbar, Filferro and Transparència reflect the state but are switched on from the menu; the Mesura (Measure) button is reserved and does nothing. The two buttons in the «Estil» (Style) row of the views box do nothing either: use the menu.
  • No properties sheet for selected elements.
  • Very complex models may take a while to load: each group or component becomes its own meshes.

13 · Questions

FAQ
Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The .skp file is read and processed inside your browser. Only Three.js and the viewer's fonts are downloaded from the network.

Why does the model sit on the ground even if it was higher or lower in SketchUp?

On loading, guaitaSKP brings the lowest part of the geometry to elevation 0 and centres it horizontally. The axes, however, are drawn at the origin the file originally had, so they may end up above or below the ground.

Why is green the Y axis and blue the vertical?

Because that is SketchUp's convention: red X, green Y and blue Z (the vertical axis). Internally the viewer works with Y as the vertical axis, but the triad colours follow SketchUp's.

I unchecked a layer and its elements came back when I pressed “Mostra-ho tot” (Show all). Is that normal?

Yes: Mostra-ho tot re-enables all hidden elements, including those of unchecked layers. The checkbox in the list is not re-ticked by itself in this version; tick it by hand so it matches what you see.

I cannot see the ground.

Check that Visualitza → Terra is ticked: it is on by default, but if you have unticked it you will only see the sky.

Can I measure distances?

Not in this version. The Mesura (Measure) button on the toolbar is a placeholder for a future feature.
